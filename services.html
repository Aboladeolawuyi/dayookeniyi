Our Services

Comprehensive, ethical and results-driven real estate services delivered with integrity, expertise and excellence.

At Shelter Nest Realty, we provide a broad range of professional real estate services designed to support individuals, corporate organizations, financial institutions and government agencies. Our approach combines technical competence, market intelligence and strict adherence to professional standards.

Valuation for All Purposes

We provide accurate and reliable valuation services for land, buildings, plant and machinery, furniture, fittings and equipment.

  • Mortgage & loan security valuation
  • Sale and purchase valuation
  • Insurance valuation
  • Compensation & compulsory acquisition
  • Rating, taxation & asset revaluation

Property & Facility Management

We deliver end-to-end management of residential, commercial and industrial properties to protect asset value and optimize returns.

  • Tenant selection & lease administration
  • Rent review and collection
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Statutory charges & service bills management

Estate Agency (Sales & Letting)

Our agency services cover property sales, letting and brokerage for private clients, corporate bodies and financial institutions.

  • Property marketing & buyer sourcing
  • Negotiation & transaction support
  • Letting of residential & commercial properties
  • Asset disposal & foreclosure sales

Property Marketing

We deploy strategic traditional and digital marketing techniques to ensure maximum exposure and optimal transaction outcomes.

  • Professional property presentation
  • Online & digital marketing campaigns
  • Targeted investor & tenant outreach

Feasibility & Viability Appraisal

We provide pre-investment appraisal services to guide informed development and investment decisions.

  • Market & demand analysis
  • Financial modelling & cash-flow projections
  • Risk assessment & development alternatives

Real Estate Advisory Services

Our advisory services support acquisition, development, portfolio optimization and investment restructuring.

  • Highest & best use studies
  • Development strategy formulation
  • Asset repositioning & portfolio review

Title Search & Verification

We conduct thorough land searches and documentation verification to safeguard clients against fraud and defective titles.

  • Land registry searches
  • Verification of survey plans & approvals
  • Identification of encumbrances

Project Management

We coordinate and manage real estate development projects from conception to completion.

  • Planning, scheduling & cost control
  • Consultant & contractor coordination
  • Quality assurance & delivery oversight
