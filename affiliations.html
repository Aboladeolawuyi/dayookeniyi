Our firm maintains affiliations with respected national and international professional bodies, reflecting our commitment to ethical standards, professional excellence, and global best practices in real estate.

NIESV

The Nigerian Institution of Estate Surveyors and Valuers is the foremost professional body regulating and promoting best practices within Nigeria’s landed profession.

ESVARBON

The statutory regulatory body responsible for licensing, ethical compliance, and professional discipline of estate surveyors and valuers in Nigeria.

RICS

A globally recognized professional body advancing excellence and standards in land, property, construction, and infrastructure.

FIG

The International Federation of Surveyors promotes professional surveying standards and sustainable land governance worldwide.
